1)Start from the initial node.
2)Add the starting node to the queue.
3)Calculate:
4)f(n)=g(n)+h(n)
5)Here:
6)g(n) = actual cost from the starting node.
h(n) = estimated cost to reach the destination.
f(n) = total estimated cost.
7)Select the node having the lowest f(n).
8)Add its neighbouring nodes to the queue.
9)Calculate the new cost and path for every neighbour.
10)Repeat until the destination is reached.
11)Display the optimal path and its total cost.
